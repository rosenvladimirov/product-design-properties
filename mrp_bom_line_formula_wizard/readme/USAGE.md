1.  Open a BoM in _Manufacturing → Products → Bills of Materials_.
2.  In the components list, click the `</>` icon next to a line's
    quantity column.
3.  The _Formula Editor_ wizard opens:

    - Pick a template from _Load from Template_ (optional).
    - Edit the formula in the code editor.
    - Consult the _Reference_ panel for variables and examples.
    - Click _Apply_ to save or _Clear Formula_ to remove.

A small _Formula defined_ indicator appears next to lines that have a
formula, so you can see at a glance which components are parametric.
